# hairstyle-ai: MVP-first game plan (2026-10-20)

## Thesis
Ship a web link, not an app. A Next.js PWA gates and crops the phone photo on-device, describes it once with a vision LLM into a typed profile, renders with one hosted editor (Nano Banana 2.1; Pro for the hero image) that always re-edits from the original photo, refines through a typed spec instead of free text, and exports a stylist sheet. We store nothing of the face. Reference-hair transfer, live video, native, self-hosting and job queues wait until ten friends have used the loop for a month. The research is unanimous on three points: no benchmark or product answers "does it keep my face" for hair edits, so a bake-off on consented friend photos is the real decision tool; the edit model is the only cost that matters; and the market gap is "what suits my face, then prove it" plus a refine vocabulary and a stylist handoff, which no incumbent exposes.

## What the MVP is and is not
**Is:** one platform (web), one flow (capture → consent → analyze → 5 ideas → 2-3 renders → refine chips → accept → export), one analysis vendor (Anthropic), one renderer (Google Nano Banana 2.1, Pro for the hero), one fallback renderer (OpenAI gpt-image-2.5-sunburst) behind one interface, invite-only magic-link login, consent record, no first-party photo storage, delete-everything, visible AI label, spend caps, feedback table.

**Is not:** native/Expo, live video of any kind, reference-hair transfer, multi-angle or turntable output, self-hosted models, job queue or event bus, server-side photo library, payments, public sharing, teens, hard face-shape labels, attractiveness scores, hair-specific vendor APIs.

## Roadmap
**Phase 0, walking skeleton (weeks 1-2).** Next.js App Router, TS strict, Zod 4.6.7, t3-env, better-auth 1.7.12 magic link with allowlist; consent screen with hashed versioned record; `<input type=file accept=image/* capture=user>` → createImageBitmap (HEIC is first-class: Safari 27.0, 2026-09-14, stopped converting HEIC to JPEG on file input) → OffscreenCanvas → JPEG ≤2048 px (EXIF stripped); one Claude Sonnet 5.5 structured-output analyze; one gemini-nano-banana-2.1 render with the fixed "edit only the hair, keep everything else identical" template; result into IndexedDB; delete-everything. *Exit:* 3 friends rendered a style on their own face from a link on iOS Safari and Android Chrome; a test asserts no log, row or trace can carry image bytes; measured cost per session under $0.60.

**Phase 1, suggest and refine (weeks 3-6).** MediaPipe tasks-vision 1.1.1 in a classic Web Worker: capture gate (one face, |yaw|,|pitch| < 15°, face ≥ 15% of frame, eyes open), head-and-shoulders crop, hair mask, current-hair descriptors; versioned taxonomy with IDs; profile confirm; suggest 5 → pick → 2-3 candidates → VLM judge → refine chips mapped to a RefinementIntent enum → re-render from the original; thumbs + reason; per-user caps, kill switch; Langfuse without inputs. *Exit:* 10 friends onboarded; ≥ 60% of sessions reach an accepted look in ≤ 3 refine turns; ≥ 80% "still me" thumbs; refusal fallback exercised.

**Phase 2, stylist handoff, friends, bake-off (weeks 7-9).** Export sheet with gemini-3-pro-image hero, stylist reply link, friends poll on your own looks, gpt-image-2.5-sunburst as second provider, pixel paste-back outside the hair mask, offline bake-off harness, providers.md and DPIA. *Exit:* two real salon visits used the sheet; blind A/B ≥ 70% "still me" and ≥ 60% "the style I asked for"; renderer choice recorded in an ADR with numbers.

**Phase 3, time-boxed experiments (weeks 10-14).** Reference-hair transfer, on-device recolor preview, optional turntable export, durable runner only if measurement demands it, passkeys. *Exit:* each experiment ships behind a flag only if it passes the Phase 2 bars on ≥ 20 pairs; otherwise it is written up and shelved.

## Product and UX
**Capture guidance.** Oval overlay, good/bad example photos, selfie framing enforced (one large frontal face, hair not cut off, hat and glasses off); the on-device gate asks for a retake instead of guessing; optional second "hair-down/side" photo when the first hides the hairline. OS photo picker, never library permission.

**Consent.** A BIPA-grade "Face data notice" before the camera: purpose; what is processed (the photo, plus on-device landmarks that are never stored or sent); who receives it by name (Anthropic for description, Google for rendering, OpenAI only if the fallback runs); retention in plain words ("we keep nothing; Google keeps abuse logs up to 55 days; Anthropic does not keep the image past the request"); no sale, ads, identification or training; delete anytime. Affirmative checkbox stored as a hashed, versioned record; adults-only self-declaration; re-consent when vendors change.

**Suggestion and refine loop.** The user confirms the profile (curl pattern 1A-4C picture chooser, stretched length, undertone swatch, a style lane the user chooses rather than one we infer). Five named ideas with "why" in balance language (adds height, softens the jaw), never a face-shape verdict or a score. The chosen idea renders 2-3 candidates; the result screen is a before/after slider plus chips ("shorter", "more curl", "less volume", "5-minute version", "keep the cut, change the colour") and free text parsed into the same enum. Each refine re-renders from the original photo with the updated spec; "no change, that contradicts" is a legal answer.

**Stylist handoff.** One-tap sheet: hero image, before, a 3-line ask in stylist vocabulary (guard numbers, length in cm), current-hair description, maintenance, visible "AI-edited preview" label. The share link lets the stylist reply Can do / Let's talk / Not yet.

**Friends.** Allowlist invites and a poll link on your own looks only. No feed.

## AI pipeline
**On-device analysis (Phase 1).** MediaPipe Face Landmarker (478 points, blendshapes, pose matrix, 3.76 MB) and hair segmenter (0.78 MB) with the 13.5 MB wasm, self-hosted with long cache headers, Apache-2.0. Prototype numbers on CPU wasm: still analysis ~150 ms, landmarker 25-34 ms/frame in a worker. Outputs: gate verdict, crop box, hair mask, coarse current-hair descriptors, soft geometry ratios injected into the prompt as text. Landmark-only face-shape rules were low-signal in the prototype, so shape is never a hard class.

**Vision-LLM analysis and suggestion.** Claude Sonnet 5.5 with structured outputs: a flat, all-required FaceHairProfile with unknown/not_visible enums, per-field confidence and provenance (measured | model | user). Sonnet and Haiku are not Covered Models, so image inputs are ephemeral and content is not retained by default (platform docs read 2026-10-20); Fable 5.1 and Mythos carry mandatory 30-day retention, so they are avoided. Suggest is text-only; refine runs on Haiku 5.5. Prototype cost: ~$0.12/session all-Sonnet, under $0.01 on Haiku. Guardrails: never identify; never infer gender, age or ethnicity (child → refuse); stylist-reviewed coily/curly bank; one image-left/right convention.

**Image-editing providers.** Primary: gemini-nano-banana-2.1 on the paid tier, $0.034 per 1K image, $0.05 at 2K, input image $0.00168 (Vertex pricing page, verified 2026-10-20); up to 14 reference images; SynthID + C2PA. Hero: gemini-3-pro-image, $0.134 at 1K-2K (verified). Fallback and A/B: gpt-image-2.5-sunburst via images/edits at medium quality (~$0.013 per 1024² output per fal's table, unverified; plus ~$0.008 per reference image, unverified; true alpha mask; 30-day abuse logs). Only the 2.5 ids are configured because gpt-image-1 shuts down 2026-10-23 and 1.5/mini on 2026-12-01. Later private path: FLUX 3 Image via fal with inline images and X-Fal-Store-IO: 0, or self-hosted FLUX 3 Image Klein (5.6B, Kommunity License below US$5M revenue per secondary sources; API $0.009/1K, unverified). Not chosen: YouCam API (~$0.09-0.11 per hairstyle render at an unverified $0.047-0.055/unit, 30-day retention), BFL direct (training terms unread), Seedream 5.0 Pro (86-110 s, SG/MY processing).

**Provider-agnostic interface.** `HairEditProvider.render({original, hairSpec, instruction, referenceHair?, mask?, boxes?, size}) → {image, providerMeta, safetyOutcome, measuredCost, latencyMs}`. AI SDK 7 (ai 7.0.146) documents generateImage with prompt.images/mask and supportsFileInputs/supportsMaskInputs flags on main (Google: images yes, masks no; OpenAI: up to 16 images plus mask); confirm the released package. Model ids, prices and shutdown dates live in one typed config with a test that fails after a shutdown date.

**Candidate ranking.** 2-3 candidates → Haiku judge scores instruction-following, over-editing, naturalness and artifacts 0-10 plus a face-unchanged check → best shown first, others one tap away. Phase 2 adds pixel paste-back outside a feathered hair+forehead mask. ArcFace identity scoring stays in the offline harness (InsightFace models are non-commercial).

**Refusals and fallbacks.** IMAGE_SAFETY / PROHIBITED_CONTENT (Google) and moderation_blocked (OpenAI) become a typed outcome; chain NB 2.1 → NB Pro → gpt-image-2.5; then an honest "the models declined this photo" with retake tips. Refusal rate is a tracked metric.

## Stretch features: honest verdict
**Reference-hair transfer ("her hair on me").** Feasible as a still-photo experiment, unproven in quality: no systematic test exists for any 2026 model. Plan: describe-then-generate (the VLM turns the reference into a HairSpec; send original + a hair-only crop of the reference made on device + spec to Nano Banana 2.1; one attribute per pass; bald-first when the target is shorter). HairFastGAN is the only runnable specialist (MIT code, non-commercial StyleGAN2 weights, aligned crops); HairPort and Stable-Hair v2 are research-only. Verdict: Phase 3 experiment; ship only if it passes the bake-off.

**Live video.** Photoreal haircut replacement on a live feed does not exist in 2026: no product ships it, YouCam live is preset overlays, and Decart's real-time models ($0.02/s, sub-40 ms claim, unverified) have no hair capability and change identity by design. Feasible and private: on-device hair recolor via the MediaPipe mask (7-15 fps measured on CPU wasm, GPU delegate on phones unmeasured) and a 4-8 s turntable clip from the chosen still (~$0.30-0.60, secondary). Verdict: ship recolor labelled "preview", skip live cuts, keep the server API client-agnostic so a native shell could reuse it if this ever becomes the core.

## Architecture and engineering practices
**State machines: yes, three small ones, no framework.** (1) Client Session as one discriminated-union type with a pure exhaustive reducer and table tests: idle → consented → captured → gated → analyzing → confirming → suggesting → rendering → reviewing → refining → accepted → exported. (2) Photo lifecycle: Picked → Prepared → ConsentChecked → Sent → Received → Discarded, with Discarded the only non-user terminal. (3) Server render job: queued → running → succeeded | failed | refused | cancelled as a guarded transition table. Promote to XState v5 (5.33.3) only if parallel regions appear (live viewfinder plus upload); never XState 6 alpha.

**Typing discipline.** TS strict with noUncheckedIndexedAccess and exactOptionalPropertyTypes; ts-reset; t3-env; Zod 4 at every boundary (request bodies, provider responses, model outputs); branded ids including a PhotoBytes type that cannot appear in log or event schemas; exhaustive-switch lint; versioned schemas with raw model text persisted; taxonomy IDs instead of names; your own validate-and-retry loop (AI SDK Output.* does not repair schema failures).

**Event-driven: no.** Request/response route handlers, a renders table and an append-only events table (ids, sizes, costs, outcomes; never bytes) are enough. No bus, CQRS, event sourcing or Temporal. One durable runner (Inngest Free, 50k runs/month, verified; or Vercel Workflow DevKit, stable since 2026-09-30) only when fan-out or >60 s providers force it.

**"Zero comments" should mean** no comments that restate code or types; names, types and tests carry the what. Keep three kinds: why (link the ADR), invariants ("never log bytes"), and the privacy statements CLAUDE.md already mandates wherever photos are captured, stored or transmitted. No commented-out code; no TODO without an issue.

**Repo conventions for AI-assisted coding.** CLAUDE.md ≤ 60 lines of non-derivable rules; docs/decisions ADRs; docs/privacy/data-flow.md and providers.md with dated verbatim vendor clauses; .claude/rules scoped to lib/privacy/**; hooks that run typecheck, lint and tests after edits; /code-review before every PR; small kebab-case modules with colocated *.test.ts and *.test-d.ts; bug fixes land with a failing-then-passing test; .env.example; golden-set fixtures keyed by (model, prompt_version, image_id, seed); a test asserting no log or event schema can carry image data.

**Observability and evals.** @ai-sdk/otel → Langfuse Cloud Hobby (free, 50k units/month, verified 2026-10-20) with recordInputs/recordOutputs=false; per-render cost ledger from measured usage; feedback table from day one; evals in earnest after ~50 real sessions.

**Spend caps.** Provider-side limits (Anthropic console; Gemini Tier 1 $250 cap and $10 per 10 minutes, secondary; OpenAI Build $500 cap, secondary); per-user daily cap (default 25 renders) and per-session cap in the DB; global kill-switch env var; Upstash rate limit by user id; alerts at 50% and 80% of a $40/month budget.

## Privacy and legal posture
Three tiers; the MVP ships Tier 0 (device-only decoding, EXIF strip, gating, cropping, landmarks) and Tier 1 (ephemeral cloud rendering, results on device); Tier 2 (encrypted auto-expiring library) is deferred. Never the Gemini free tier (it trains and allows human review); attach billing before the first photo. Landmarks are treated as biometric personal data under the ICO reading but not Article 9 data (not for identification); never persisted or transmitted. BIPA-shaped consent (an electronic signature is a written release; one recovery per person per method after P.A. 103-769; the MAC try-on motion to dismiss was denied in June 2026, date unverified) also satisfies Washington MHMDA and Colorado's notice-consent-destroy model; publish a retention schedule. EU AI Act Art. 50 applies since 2026-08-02; the friends' personal use is outside 50(4), but the stylist export is professional-facing, so every output carries a visible "AI-edited preview" label and provider marks are preserved. Design out non-consensual editing: own face only, single face, reference photos hair-cropped on device, no renders of others, adults only, in-app report/flag. TAKE IT DOWN: an invite-only app likely is not a covered platform; implement removal anyway. A light DPIA lives in the repo. Consent is shaped to Apple DPLA 3.3.3(K) and Guideline 5.1.2(i) even on web, so a native shell would not need a redesign.

# hairstyle-ai: a quality-first game plan (2026-10-20)

## Thesis
Build "what suits my face, proven on my own photo," not another catalog. Quality means three measurable things: the render is still the user, the suggestion fits this face and this hair (including coily textures and men's medium/long cuts), and refinement converges instead of drifting. No published benchmark measures any of this for the October-2026 editors (the hair-edit lane found zero independent controlled identity tests), so the first deliverable is an evaluation harness and a provider bake-off, and every later choice is gated by it. Multi-candidate fan-out, a Pro-tier hero render and a frontier VLM for the stylist brain cost more per session, but at ten friends that is still about $30-35 a month.

## MVP: is and is not
**Is:** a web PWA; selfie capture with an on-device quality gate; one explicit face-data consent; on-device geometry plus one vision-LLM pass into a typed `FaceHairProfile` the user confirms; 3-5 suggestions from a versioned catalog, filtered deterministically, ranked and explained by the LLM; each suggestion rendered as several candidates on two providers, auto-ranked, best shown; one-attribute-per-pass refine driven by a typed intent; an export card (reference image with a visible "AI preview" label plus a plain-language stylist script); invite-only friend votes.

**Is not:** live video try-on; reference-hair transfer (a Phase 4 experiment); a native app; free-form colour edits (colour is its own pass); attractiveness scores; group photos; under-18 users; monetization.

## Roadmap
**Phase 0, harness first (weeks 1-2).** Scaffold repo and conventions; assemble the golden set (consented friend photos, SFHQ synthetic subjects); implement the metric stack and two provider adapters. Exit: a frozen 20-30 image golden set with hashes scores end to end; CI runs the synthetic subset.

**Phase 1, bake-off (weeks 3-5).** 10-20 selfies x 10 styles x providers (Nano Banana 2.1, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst with and without mask, FLUX 3 Image via fal or Replicate, a FLUX 3 Image Klein self-host trial, Seedream 5.0 Pro, one of Krea 3 or Ideogram 4.5, YouCam hair-transfer on free units), two seeds each, plus a friends' blind A/B. Exit: a ranked table of identity pass rate, outside-mask change, VLM rubric, refusal rate, p50/p95 latency and measured cost; primary and fallback chosen; prompt template v1 frozen.

**Phase 2, vertical slice (weeks 6-9).** Capture gate, consent, analysis, profile confirm, suggest, fan-out render, rank, review, one refine, export. Exit: five friends finish a session unaided; at least 80% of sessions produce a candidate rated 4+ on "would show a stylist"; at least 90% of shipped candidates pass the identity floor; p95 time to first candidate under 60 s.

**Phase 3, convergence and coverage (weeks 10-13).** `RefinementIntent` machine with contradiction detection, bald-first chaining, paste-back compositing, a stylist-reviewed coily and curly suggestion bank, handoff sheet with reply link, friend votes. Exit: at least 70% of refine sessions end in acceptance within three rounds; the textured-hair subset meets the same thresholds as the rest; one friend takes an export to a real stylist and reports back.

**Phase 4, stretch experiments (weeks 14+),** each gated by the harness: reference-hair transfer, on-device live recolor with freeze-frame, an optional turntable clip, an optional self-hosted Klein as the private rendering path.

## Product and UX
**Capture.** Oval overlay, live lighting meter, auto-capture when the on-device gate passes (one face, yaw and pitch within about 10-15 degrees, face at least 15% of frame, eyes open, hair not cut off by the frame); ask for a retake rather than guess. Request an optional hair-pulled-back shot (hairline, ears) and a head-and-shoulders shot. Flag glasses and hats from the multiclass segmenter's "others" class. Treat HEIC as first-class input (Safari 27.0, 2026-09-14, stopped converting it to JPEG); downscale on device to 1536-2048 px with EXIF stripped.

**Consent.** A BIPA-grade face-data notice before the camera opens: purpose, what runs on device, which named vendors receive a crop and how long they keep abuse logs, that the relay keeps nothing, no sale, ads, identification or training, and a delete path; an affirmative checkbox as the electronic-signature written release; a hashed, versioned consent record. Apple's DPLA 3.3.3(K) wants the same two consents (collection and use, off-device transfer).

**Suggest and refine.** Show face shape as "between oval and oblong," never a hard label; both prototypes saw hard labels flip across crops and expressions. Each suggestion carries a taxonomy ID, a one-line why, styling minutes, grow-out and a boldness 1-5. Refine with chips mapped to a `RefinementIntent` enum (shorter, more curl, less side volume, five-minute version), one attribute per pass, colour and beard as separate passes; detect contradictions and offer "no change." Every refine re-renders from the original photo with the accepted output as a reference, so drift cannot accumulate: the spec converges, not the pixels.

**Stylist handoff.** Reference image with a visible "AI-edited preview" label and SynthID/C2PA intact, the `HairDescription` rendered as a plain-language ask-for script (guard numbers where relevant), the current-hair description, and a reply link (Can do / Let's talk / Not yet).

**Friends.** Invite-only accounts, A/B vote links, no public feed; people share only their own renders.

## AI pipeline
**On-device (MediaPipe tasks-vision 1.1.1, Apache-2.0, in a classic Web Worker).** Face Landmarker (478 points, blendshapes, pose matrix), the 0.78 MB hair segmenter, and the 16 MB multiclass segmenter once per still, masks unioned and dilated. Outputs: capture gate, head-and-shoulders crop, hair mask, hairline-based face length, repeatable ratios, current-hair descriptors. Nothing persists; landmarks never leave the device. Known gaps: the hair segmenter fires on fur and fabric and misses grey hair on light backgrounds; multiclass IoU is 71.9 on Monk tones 9-10 versus 81.7 on tones 1-2, so the eval set over-samples dark skin with textured hair.

**Vision-LLM analysis and suggestion (Claude structured outputs).** One image pass yields a flat, all-required profile with `unknown`/`not_visible` enum members, per-field confidence and provenance (measured, model, user); measured ratios go in as text. Suggest and refine are text-only. Quality-first uses Opus 5.5 for analyze and suggest (prototype estimate about $0.24 per session with two refines; Sonnet 5.5 about $0.12) and Haiku 5.5 as judge. Avoid Fable 5.1: it is a Covered Model with mandatory 30-day retention and no ZDR unless authorized (platform doc, 2026-10-20). Never infer gender, age, ethnicity or attractiveness; the user picks a style lane and a curl pattern from pictures.

**Image editing behind one `HairEditProvider`** (original crop, optional reference, optional mask or boxes, typed description, keep-list, seed; returns image, metadata, safety outcome, measured cost). # hairstyle-ai: privacy-first game plan (2026-10-20)

## 1. Thesis

The most private design that still works: every pixel-level step (face detection, pose gate, hair mask, crop, current-hair description) runs on the phone; exactly one downscaled, EXIF-stripped head-and-shoulders crop leaves the device per session; it goes only to vendors with no-training terms and ephemeral or disable-able retention; the app itself never stores a face. Analysis goes to Claude Haiku/Sonnet 5.5 (image inputs ephemeral, no training). Rendering goes to Nano Banana 2.1 on Vertex AI (contractual no-training, 24-hour cache disabled, region pinned) with a self-hosted FLUX 3 Image Klein "private mode" built in Phase 3 and promoted to default if it passes the bake-off. Results live in the browser.

What this trades away, stated up front: no OpenAI alpha-mask editing by default, no Seedream reference transfer, a 10% Vertex regional surcharge, an unproven open model and GPU ops if Klein wins, and both stretch features in reduced form (reference transfer becomes "inspired by this hair" from a hair-only crop; live video becomes on-device recolor).

## 2. What the MVP is and is not

**Is:** an invite-only web PWA; guided capture with an on-device quality gate; a BIPA-grade consent before the camera opens; on-device MediaPipe landmarks, hair mask and crop; one Claude call producing a typed FaceHairProfile the user confirms; a typed catalog plus LLM ranking giving 4-6 ideas with a plain-language "why"; the top three rendered on the user's own photo, always re-edited from the original; refine chips mapped to a typed HairSpec; a stylist card (render + "ask for" text + visible AI-edited label); device-only history and "delete everything".

**Is not:** a native app; live photoreal restyle; reference-photo hair transfer (Phase 3 experiment); self-hosted GPU (Phase 3); friends voting (Phase 3); colour/season analysis; attractiveness scores; under-18s; public sharing; payments; turntable video; OpenAI as a default renderer.

## 3. Roadmap

**Phase 0, week 1 - decide and scaffold.** ADRs for platform, providers, privacy tiers, state model and runner; CLAUDE.md under 60 lines; docs/privacy/data-flow.md and providers.md with dated verbatim vendor clauses; DPIA-lite; Next.js scaffold with strict TS, Zod 4.6.7, CI; Vertex project with billing, `disableCache=true`, abuse-monitoring exception requested; Anthropic spend limit; Langfuse with input/output recording off. *Exit:* hello-PWA opens the camera on iOS 27.0.1 (Safari and home-screen) and Android; a type test proves no log or event can carry image bytes.

**Phase 1, weeks 2-4 - capture, analyse, suggest; no rendering.** Worker-hosted MediaPipe gate and crop; HEIC decode; consent screen and record; analyse-confirm-suggest with structured outputs; catalog v1 with taxonomy IDs. *Exit:* five friends finish the flow on their phones; the gate bounces the prototype's two bad-photo classes; two independent analysis calls agree on 80% of enum fields and 4/6 taxonomy IDs; zero image bytes anywhere server-side.

**Phase 2, weeks 5-8 - render, refine, hand off.** HairEditProvider with Vertex Nano Banana 2.1 primary and fal-hosted FLUX 3 Image fallback; jobs table plus Inngest Free or Trigger.dev Free when the pipeline becomes multi-step; refine loop; on-device paste-back and candidate pre-gate; stylist card; feedback table; spend caps; bake-off on consented photos. *Exit:* blind friend A/B median "still me" and "style I asked for" both >= 4/5; p95 render under 30 s; cost per session <= $0.60; bake-off ADR written and provider/prompt frozen.

**Phase 3, weeks 9-12 - private mode and honest stretch features.** Self-hosted Klein adapter scored against Nano Banana 2.1; "inspired by" reference feature; on-device live recolor; private friends vote links; opt-in encrypted library; passkeys. *Exit:* Klein becomes default only if identity pass rate is within 5 points and median friend rating within 0.5; recolor reaches 15 fps on one mid-range Android and one iPhone or is cut; 60% of reference pairs rated "recognisably that hair".

**Phase 4, ongoing.** Quarterly re-read of vendor clauses; deprecation watch (gpt-image-1 shuts 2026-10-23; gpt-image-1.5/mini 2026-12-01; Nano Banana 2 no date); golden-set eval on every prompt or model change; AUP revision check on 2026-11-12.

## 4. Product and UX

**Capture guidance.** Oval overlay, live lighting meter, auto-capture when the gate passes: one face, |yaw| and |pitch| under 15 degrees from the transformation matrix, face at least 15% of the frame, sharpness, eyes open, hair not cut off. Ask for a retake instead of guessing (two of five prototype photos deserved one). Offer an optional hair-pulled-back shot for hairline and a shoulders-in-frame shot for neck length. Ask the user to remove hats and glasses when the multiclass "others" class fires.

**Consent.** A face-data notice before the camera: purpose; what runs on device; which vendors receive the crop (named); retention ("our relay keeps nothing; vendor abuse logs up to N days; results stay on your phone"); no sale, ads, identification or training; how to withdraw; an affirmative checkbox as the electronic-signature written release. Friends choose their provider allowlist here. Store a hashed, versioned consent record. Adults only by self-attestation.

**Suggestion and refine loop.** Show the confirmed profile first ("chin-length dark brown bob with a fringe; face between oval and oblong") with every field editable; face shape is a soft top-2, never a hard label. Ideas arrive as cards with boldness 1-5, styling minutes, maintenance, grow-out, and a factual "why it suits" in balance language. Render the top three with a progressive reveal over the source photo. Refine chips ("shorter", "more curl", "5-minute version", "bolder", "colour only", "what would a stylist say") map to a RefinementIntent that edits the HairSpec and re-renders from the original; contradictory asks get a "no change" option or a question.

**Stylist handoff.** Render plus plain-language "ask for" sheet (cut, length in finger-widths or centimetres, layers, fringe, texture, finish, grow-out), current-hair description, and the AI-edited label; exported from the device as an image or PDF.

**Friends features (Phase 3).** Private A/B vote links between accounts, subject-controlled, with a report control and removal path. No feed, no public links.

## 5. AI pipeline

**On-device analysis.** @mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.1.1 (Apache-2.0) in a classic Web Worker (module workers fail), self-hosted wasm (13.5 MB), landmarker (3.76 MB) and hair segmenter (0.78 MB), service-worker cached. Viewfinder at 640x480 in VIDEO mode (about 34 ms per frame for landmarks on a 4-core CPU); the captured still gets landmarks, hair-segmenter and multiclass masks unioned and dilated, a head-and-shoulders crop, repeatable ratios (hairline-based length/width, jaw angle, canonical-relative deviations) and current-hair descriptors, in about 150 ms. Gate the hair mask on "a face was found and the component touches the head" because the segmenter fires on fur and fabric. Landmarks are discarded after use. Known fairness gap: multiclass IoU 71.9 on Monk tones 9-10 versus 81.7 on tones 1-2, so the golden set must over-weight those subjects.

**Vision-LLM analysis and suggestion.** One Claude Haiku 5.5 call (Sonnet 5.5 if JSON quality demands) on a 1024-1280 px crop through a backend proxy, structured output, flat all-required schema with `unknown`/`not_visible` members, per-field confidence and provenance, measured ratios injected as text. Never infer gender, age, ethnicity, emotion or attractiveness; the user chooses a style lane. Suggest and refine are text-only calls against a versioned catalog (shape-up's 50 classes plus textured and men's medium/long additions, headcutter's schema), with a deterministic feasibility filter before LLM ranking and at most one flagged wildcard.

**Image editing providers and the interface.** `HairEditProvider.render({original, keepList, hairSpec, referenceHairCrop?, boxes?, priorAccepted?}) -> {image, providerMeta, safetyOutcome, cost}` with capability flags (maxReferenceImages, supportsMask, supportsBoxes, retentionClass, residency). AI SDK 7.0.146 documents `generateImage` with `prompt.images`/`prompt.mask` and per-model flags (Google: images yes, mask no; BFL flux-3-image: 10 references, no mask; OpenAI: 16 images plus mask), but the SDK does not reject unsupported inputs, so our interface enforces them. Adapters: `vertex-nano-banana-2.1` ($0.034/1K, $0.05/2K, $0.113/4K plus $0.00168 per input image, Vertex page 2026-10-20, +10% non-global), `fal-flux-3-image` (inline images, `X-Fal-Store-IO: 0`, `X-Fal-Object-Lifecycle-Preference`; fal price for FLUX 3 edit not found), `nano-banana-pro` for the hero render ($0.134), and in Phase 3 `self-hosted-klein`. Never the BFL direct API until its training terms are read; never the Gemini free tier.

**Candidate ranking.** Two candidates per look. On device: outside-hair-mask SSIM and landmark displacement floors against the original, hair-region change to reject no-ops, then composite original pixels back outside the dilated mask union. ArcFace stays in the offline harness (InsightFace models are non-commercial). A Claude VLM judge is optional per candidate.

**Refusals and fallbacks.** Map `IMAGE_SAFETY`/`PROHIBITED_CONTENT` (Google), `moderation_blocked` (OpenAI) and self-host NSFW flags to a SafetyOutcome enum; fall back along the user's consented provider list; never retry minors; show retake guidance. Rules with evidence behind them: one attribute per pass, bald-first when the target is shorter or tighter, re-edit from the original (Nano Banana drifts by about the fourth chained edit), explicit keep-list every time.

## 6. Stretch features

**Reference-hair transfer.** Feasible as a still-photo feature; no systematic test exists for any current model. Privacy-first shape: crop the reference to hair on device (the other person's face never uploads), have the VLM write a HairSpec from it, then send [user crop, hair crop, spec] to the default renderer (Nano Banana 2.1 takes up to 14 references; FLUX 3 and Klein up to 10). ## 9. Cost model

Per user per month at 3 sessions of 12 renders plus a hero render: 36 x ~$0.039 (Nano Banana 2.1 at 1K, input image, +10% region) = $1.40; 3 Pro hero renders ~$0.45; analysis and text on Sonnet 5.5 ~$0.36 (Haiku ~$0.02). Total about $1.9, range $1.4-2.3. Ten friends: $14-23 in model spend, $0 hosting on free tiers (Vercel/Cloudflare, R2, Supabase, Langfuse, Inngest/Trigger.dev), plus $0-20 for the optional self-hosted Klein GPU (unverified; GPU rates $1.59-3.95/hr are secondary and seconds per edit unmeasured). ## 10. Test data and evaluation

Tier 0 consented friend photos (10-15 per person, consent manifest, gitignored, EXIF stripped); Tier 1 ~50 SFHQ synthetic subjects (MIT) as the only shippable CI set, chosen to cover Monk 9-10 with textured hair, grey hair on light backgrounds, 1A-4C, men's medium/long, hats, glasses, truncated long hair; Tier 2 research datasets offline only, FairFace (CC BY 4.0) for bias probes. Freeze a hashed golden set; key every render by (model, prompt_version, image_id, seed). Bake-off: 10-20 selfies x 10 styles across Nano Banana 2.1, Pro, FLUX 3 Image with and without boxes, Klein, gpt-image-2.5-sunburst medium offline; score ArcFace identity with per-person floors, outside-hair-mask SSIM/LPIPS, DINO hair similarity, latency, cost, refusal rate. Verify the BiSeNet hair index (17 vs 13) before trusting mask metrics. Consistency probe with two independent analysis calls per photo scored on enums and taxonomy IDs. Friend protocol: blind A/B, 5 raters, ~20 originals, three 1-5 questions, failure tags, bootstrap CIs, Bradley-Terry.
