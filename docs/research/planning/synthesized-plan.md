# hairstyle-ai: final game plan (2026-10-20)

## 1. Thesis and positioning

Position the app as "find what suits my face, prove it on my own photo, then hand it to a stylist", not another catalog. The market default is upload, pick from 10-250 presets, pay; no incumbent exposes a refine vocabulary, a suitability-first flow with rendering as proof, a stylist handoff with a reply link, or honest coverage of textured hair and men's medium/long cuts. Those gaps are what a private friends app can own.

The base is the MVP-first plan (all three judges: two-week walking skeleton, single TypeScript runtime, no bucket, no queue), with the grafts the judges made mandatory: Vertex AI with caching disabled as the only Google path; OpenAI as an opt-in fallback, never the default; an on-device candidate pre-gate plus union-mask paste-back instead of a Haiku-only face check; the top three ideas rendered on the user's face before any choice; refine re-rendered from the original with the accepted candidate as a reference; and the quality-first harness (hashed golden set, textured-hair subset, prompt change = PR with harness deltas) from Phase 2.

Three evidence facts drive every choice. No independent controlled identity test exists for any October-2026 editor, so friends' consented photos and a blind A/B are the only decision tool. The edit model is the only cost that matters; analysis is cents. Live photoreal haircut replacement does not exist in 2026, so "live" means on-device recolor, labelled as such.

## 2. MVP scope and what is deferred

**In:** web PWA opened from a link (iOS Safari, Android Chrome); invite-only magic-link login, adults-only self-declaration; layered face-data consent before capture; file-input capture with HEIC-first decoding, EXIF stripped, 2048 px max on device; on-device MediaPipe gate, head-and-shoulders crop, hair mask, current-hair descriptors; one Claude Sonnet 5.5 analyze call into a typed, user-confirmed FaceHairProfile (curl pattern, length, undertone and style lane picked by the user); five ideas with taxonomy IDs and balance-language reasons, the top three rendered on the user's face (two candidates each) with gemini-nano-banana-2.1 on Vertex; on-device pre-gate and paste-back; refine chips and free text mapped to a RefinementIntent enum, re-rendered from the original with the accepted candidate as a reference; hero render on Nano Banana Pro; export sheet (hero, before, stylist script with guard numbers and centimetres, current-hair description, maintenance, visible "AI-edited preview" label) shared through the user's own share sheet; stylist reply link carrying only a token and the typed ask; "I got the cut" before/after compare on device; delete-everything; per-user caps, kill switch, cost ledger, feedback table, Langfuse without inputs.

**Deferred:** native/Expo; live video of any kind (recolor preview is a Phase 3 experiment); reference-hair transfer (Phase 3, "inspired by", hair-only crop); multi-angle or turntable output; self-hosted models or any GPU; durable runner, event bus, CQRS, Temporal; server-side photo or render storage (the Tier 2 opt-in library is an owner decision); OpenAI as a default renderer; payments, public sharing, feeds, under-18s; hard face-shape labels and attractiveness scores; hair-specific vendor APIs (YouCam) and the BFL direct API; C2PA re-signing infrastructure.

## 3. Roadmap

**Phase 0, walking skeleton (weeks 1-2).** Next.js App Router, TS strict, Zod 4.6.7, t3-env, better-auth 1.7.12 magic link with allowlist; GCP project with billing, `cacheConfig.disableCache=true`, region decision recorded, abuse-monitoring exception requested; Anthropic spend limit and DPA; consent screen with hashed versioned record; `<input type=file accept=image/* capture=user>` → createImageBitmap (HEIC is first-class: Safari 27.0, 2026-09-14, stopped converting it to JPEG) → OffscreenCanvas → JPEG; one Sonnet 5.5 structured-output analyze; one gemini-nano-banana-2.1 render with the fixed "edit only the hair, keep everything else identical" template; result into IndexedDB; delete-everything. *Exit:* three friends render a style on their own face from a link on iOS 27 Safari and Android Chrome; a type test proves no log, event, trace or DB row can carry image bytes; measured per-render cost, latency p50/p95, refusal count and backgrounded-tab loss recorded (no source measured Nano Banana latency, so these set the Phase 1 bars); Vercel body and duration limits confirmed empirically or Cloudflare chosen.

**Phase 1, suggest, render, refine (weeks 3-6).** MediaPipe tasks-vision 1.1.1 in a classic Web Worker: gate (one face, |yaw| and |pitch| under 15 degrees, face at least 15% of frame, eyes open, hair in frame), crop, hair mask as the dilated union of the hair segmenter and the multiclass segmenter's hair class run once on the still; getUserMedia viewfinder (oval overlay, lighting meter, auto-capture) behind a flag with file input as fallback, tested in iOS 27 home-screen mode; catalog v1 with taxonomy IDs (LLM-drafted, human-reviewed, Zod-validated in CI); profile confirm; top three rendered with progressive reveal; pre-gate and paste-back; refine with accepted reference; colour and beard as separate passes; caps, kill switch, Langfuse. *Exit:* ten friends onboarded; at least 60% of sessions reach an accepted look within three refine turns; at least 80% "still me" thumbs; two independent analysis calls agree on at least 80% of enum fields and 4 of 6 taxonomy IDs; refusal fallback exercised.

**Phase 2, handoff, harness, bake-off (weeks 7-10).** Export sheet and stylist reply link; "I got the cut" compare; golden set frozen with hashes; offline harness; two-ring bake-off (friends' photos only to Vertex, Anthropic and, for opt-ins, OpenAI; SFHQ synthetic faces to FLUX 3 via fal, Klein, Seedream and one of Krea 3 or Ideogram 4.5); gpt-image-2.5-sunburst wired as the opt-in fallback; renderer and prompt frozen in an ADR with numbers; DPIA and providers.md; paid stylist review of the catalog if no stylist is in the group. *Exit:* two real salon visits used the sheet; blind A/B at least 70% "still me" and 60% "the style I asked for", with the textured-hair subset reported separately; p95 time-to-first-candidate within the Phase 0 bar.

**Phase 3, time-boxed experiments (weeks 11-15).** Reference-hair "inspired by this hair"; on-device recolor preview; friends poll via share sheet; passkeys; Tier 2 opt-in library if the owner wants it; durable runner only if measurement demands it. *Exit:* each ships behind a flag only if it passes the Phase 2 bars on at least 20 pairs; otherwise it is written up and shelved.

**Ongoing:** quarterly re-read of vendor clauses; Anthropic AUP revision check on 2026-11-12; shutdown-date test (gpt-image-1 on 2026-10-23, gpt-image-1.5/mini on 2026-12-01); harness re-run on every prompt or model change.

## 4. Product and UX flow

**Capture.** Good/bad example photos; OS picker, never library permission; the gate asks for a retake instead of guessing (two of five prototype photos deserved one); optional hair-down/side shot when the hairline is hidden; "remove hat and glasses" when the multiclass "others" class fires. Oval overlay, lighting meter and auto-capture ship only with the viewfinder flag, because a native camera UI opened by file input cannot host an overlay.

**Consent.** Layered: a short notice (what happens, who receives the crop, "we keep nothing on our servers", delete anytime) with "learn more" opening the full face-data notice: purpose; on-device landmarks never stored or sent; named recipients (Anthropic for description and preview checks; Google Cloud Vertex AI for rendering; OpenAI only if the user switches on the fallback); retention in plain words with N filled only from primary pages ("Google keeps abuse logs up to N days; Anthropic does not keep the image past the request but may keep flagged content up to 2 years"); no sale, ads, identification or training; affirmative checkbox stored as a hashed, versioned record next to the notice text; adults only; re-consent when the vendor list changes. One optional toggle: "allow a second renderer (OpenAI) if Google declines".

**Profile and ideas.** The user confirms every field: picture-based curl pattern (1A-4C plus mixed), stretched length, undertone swatch (photo undertone is unreliable), style lane. Face shape reads "between oval and oblong", never a hard class (labels flipped across crops in the prototype). Five ideas with boldness 1-5, styling minutes, maintenance, grow-out and a balance-language "why" (adds height, softens the jaw); never a score.

**Render and refine.** The top three ideas render on the user's face with a progressive reveal over the source photo; before/after slider; the other two ideas one tap away. Chips ("shorter", "more curl", "less side volume", "5-minute version", "keep the cut, change the colour", "what would a stylist say") and free text parse into one RefinementIntent enum; one attribute per pass; "no change, that contradicts" is a legal answer; every refine re-renders from the original with the accepted candidate attached as a reference, so the spec converges and the pixels never chain.

**Handoff and return.** One-tap sheet with hero, before, a three-line ask in stylist vocabulary, current-hair description, maintenance and the visible label; the user shares it through the share sheet; the reply link (Can do / Let's talk / Not yet) carries only a token and the typed ask, never an image. After the appointment, "I got the cut" compares the real result on device and tags what differed: the natural return visit and the only ground truth on achievability.

**Friends.** Allowlist invites; Phase 3 poll links carry votes only, images travel by share sheet. No feed.

## 5. AI pipeline and provider choices

Prices as of 2026-10-20; "verified" means read on a primary page during this sweep.

**On-device (Tier 0).** @mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.1.1 (Apache-2.0), self-hosted wasm (13.5 MB), landmarker (3.76 MB), hair segmenter (0.78 MB), multiclass (16.4 MB, lazy-loaded, once per still) in a classic Web Worker (module workers fail). Measured: still analysis about 150 ms, landmarker 25-34 ms per frame in a worker, hair mask 8-12 fps on CPU wasm; GPU delegate unmeasured on phones. Outputs: gate verdict, crop, hair mask, coarse descriptors, soft geometry ratios as prompt text. Known gaps: the segmenter fires on fur and fabric (gate on "face found and component touches the head"), is weak on grey hair on light backgrounds (hence the union), and scores 71.9 vs 81.7 IoU on Monk 9-10 vs 1-2.

**Analysis and suggestion.** Claude Sonnet 5.5 structured outputs for analyze (image in once) and suggest (text only); Haiku 5.5 for refine deltas and the candidate judge. Neither is a Covered Model, so images are ephemeral and content is not retained by default (platform docs read 2026-10-20); Fable 5.1 and Mythos carry mandatory 30-day retention and are avoided. About $0.014 per photo analysis on Sonnet (verified rates); prototype session estimate $0.12 all-Sonnet, thinking tokens the largest unknown. Schema rules: flat, all-required, additionalProperties false, no numeric min/max, unknown/not_visible enum members, per-field confidence and provenance (measured | model | user), raw text persisted, own validate-and-retry loop. Guardrails: never identify; never infer gender, age, ethnicity, emotion or attractiveness (child → refuse); user-chosen style lane; stylist-reviewed coily/curly bank; one image-left/right convention.

**Rendering.** Primary: gemini-nano-banana-2.1 on Vertex AI, $0.034 per 1K output image (1,120 tokens), $0.05 at 2K, $0.00168 per input image, verified; +10% in non-global regions; up to 14 reference images; no mask input (the SDK ImageConfig has none); SynthID. Hero: gemini-3-pro-image, $0.134 at 1K-2K, $0.00112 per input image, verified; reviewers report slightly better facial geometry (secondary). Opt-in fallback: gpt-image-2.5-sunburst via images/edits at medium quality; $5/$8/$30 per 1M tokens verified, about $0.013 per 1024-px output and about $0.008 per reference image unverified (fal table); true alpha mask; only the 2.5 ids are configured. Phase 3 private path, Ring B only until terms are read: FLUX 3 Image via fal (inline images, X-Fal-Store-IO: 0, lifecycle header; check whether fal flags it as a partner model that receives Client Content; fal price not found) or self-hosted FLUX 3 Image Klein (5.6B, 10 references, boxes, verified; Kommunity License below US$5M revenue with a s.2(e) filtering duty, secondary; API $0.009/1K unverified). Not chosen: Gemini Developer API (55-day abuse logs, ZDR eligibility unclear), BFL direct (training terms unread), Seedream 5.0 Pro (86-110 s, SG/MY processing, terms unreachable), YouCam (about $0.09-0.11 per hairstyle render at an unverified $0.047-0.055 per unit, 30-day retention).

**Interface.** `HairEditProvider.render({original, hairSpec, keepList, instruction, priorAccepted?, referenceHairCrop?, mask?, boxes?, size}) → {image, providerMeta, safetyOutcome, measuredCost, latencyMs}` with capability flags (maxReferenceImages, supportsMask, supportsBoxes, retentionClass, residency) enforced in-house, because AI SDK 7 (ai 7.0.146) documents generateImage prompt.images/mask flags on main but does not reject unsupported inputs. Mask-constrained providers get a mask dilated well beyond the current hair, since an edit cannot grow hair outside its mask. Model ids, prices, retention classes and shutdown dates live in one typed config with as_of dates and a test that fails past a shutdown date.

**Candidates, gate, paste-back.** Two candidates per look; a bald-first chain replaces one candidate when the target is shorter or tighter (two calls, costed). On device: one face found; outside-hair-mask SSIM against the original above a floor calibrated offline on the golden set (landmark displacement only as a secondary signal: the mesh is template-dominated); hair-region change above a no-op floor; then original pixels composited back outside the feathered, dilated union of the original hair mask and a hair mask computed on the output. Phase 2 adds the Haiku 5.5 rubric (instruction-following, over-editing, naturalness, artifacts, 0-10, threshold 6); consent names Anthropic as receiving previews for that check. ArcFace stays in the offline harness: InsightFace weights are non-commercial and a runtime 1:1 comparison would be biometric verification.

**Refusals.** IMAGE_SAFETY and PROHIBITED_CONTENT (Google) and moderation_blocked (OpenAI) become a typed SafetyOutcome; chain NB 2.1 → NB Pro → gpt-image-2.5 (opt-ins only) → honest decline with retake tips; refusal rate per provider is tracked. Gemini and OpenAI image endpoints accept no idempotency key, so the renders table enforces one render id per request.

## 6. Stretch features

**Reference-hair transfer ("her hair on me").** Tier 1 (still photo): feasible, quality unproven; no systematic test exists for any 2026 model. Shape: crop the reference to hair on device (the other person's face never uploads), the VLM writes a HairSpec from it, send [user crop, hair crop, spec] to Nano Banana 2.1, one attribute per pass, bald-first for tighter targets; label it "inspired by this hair"; expect hairline and texture to vary and pose gaps, braids and updos to fail. HairFastGAN (MIT code, non-commercial StyleGAN2 weights, aligned crops) is the only runnable specialist, for a Ring B second opinion; HairPort (CC BY-NC-ND, 24 GB VRAM, minutes per pair) and Stable-Hair v2 are research-only; H-Adapter and DPF have no code. Tier 2 (multi-angle stills): feasible but inconsistent; only with user-supplied side photos edited with the same spec. Verdict: Phase 3 experiment, shipped behind a flag if at least 60% of 20 or more pairs are rated "recognisably that hair" and pass the identity bars.

**Live video.** Tier 4 (live photoreal cut): not feasible in 2026. No product ships it; YouCam live is preset AR overlays; Decart's Lucy models ($0.02 per second, sub-40 ms claim, unverified) document no hair capability and change identity by design; YouCam's API has no hair video endpoint. Tier 3 (4-8 s turntable from the chosen still via Veo 3.1 Lite/Fast, $0.30-0.60 per clip, secondary): feasible but sends the render to another vendor, so off by default. What ships: on-device hair recolor from the MediaPipe mask (8-12 fps CPU floor; the GPU delegate must be measured on phones; the iOS 26.x home-screen rotation bug must be checked on iOS 27) plus freeze-frame-to-render, labelled "preview". The server API stays client-agnostic so a native shell could reuse it if live ever becomes the core.

## 7. Architecture and engineering practices

**Platform.** Next.js App Router PWA, TypeScript, on Vercel Hobby (personal, non-commercial per the official snippet; the friends case is unaddressed) with Cloudflare Workers as the drop-in if limits or terms bite; every vendor call goes through a route handler; no native app.

**State machines: yes, three small ones, no framework.** (1) Client Session as one discriminated union (idle → consented → captured → gated(ok | retake) → analyzing → confirming → suggesting → rendering → reviewing → refining → accepted → exported, plus errored) with a pure exhaustive `(state, event) => state` reducer and table tests. (2) Photo lifecycle: Picked → Prepared → ConsentChecked → Sent → Received → Discarded; Discarded is the only non-user terminal; bytes exist only between Prepared and Received; a test asserts no event carries bytes. (3) Server render job: queued → running → succeeded | failed | refused | cancelled as a guarded transition table shared as vocabulary. Promote to XState 5.33.3 only if parallel regions appear (viewfinder beside upload); never XState 6 alpha.

**Typing.** strict, noUncheckedIndexedAccess, exactOptionalPropertyTypes; ts-reset; t3-env; Zod 4 at every boundary with types inferred from schemas; branded ids (UserId, SessionId, PhotoId, RenderId, ConsentVersion, TaxonomyId) and a PhotoBytes type that can exist only inside lib/privacy and never in log, event or DB schemas (type test); exhaustive-switch lint; versioned schemas; taxonomy IDs instead of names; RefinementIntent and SafetyOutcome as enums; geometry ratios bucketed into enums before storage.

**Async jobs: none in the MVP.** One route handler per candidate (not one handler fanning out), progress over SSE, a renders row per call for idempotency, status, measured cost and outcome; Screen Wake Lock while rendering; a backgrounded tab may lose one render of about $0.04, which the client re-requests under a new id; that loss rate is measured. If it exceeds a few percent, or a provider exceeds the function limit, add one durable runner (Trigger.dev, 20 concurrent free, verified; Inngest's 5 would queue a demo evening) whose payloads carry keys into a private bucket with a 24-hour lifecycle and delete-on-completion, never bytes, and name that processor in consent. No event bus, CQRS, event sourcing or Temporal; an append-only events table (ids, sizes, costs, outcomes) is "events as data" and doubles as the eval log.

**"Zero comments" should mean** none that restate code, types or names, and no commented-out code; keep four tagged classes: `why:` (link the ADR), `invariant:`, `privacy:` (CLAUDE.md already mandates these wherever photos are captured, stored or transmitted) and `quirk:` for gotchas an assistant cannot infer (classic worker not module worker; GPU delegate needs the shared canvas; createImageBitmap decodes HEIC on Safari; iOS rotation). No TODO without an issue link.

**Repo conventions for AI-assisted coding.** CLAUDE.md of at most 60 lines holding only non-derivable rules; AGENTS.md alias; docs/decisions ADRs, each with a "what would change this" line; docs/privacy/data-flow.md and providers.md with dated verbatim vendor clauses; .claude/rules scoped to lib/privacy/** and lib/providers/**; hooks that run typecheck, lint and tests after edits; /code-review and a privacy checklist before every PR; small kebab-case modules with colocated *.test.ts and *.test-d.ts; bug fixes land with a failing-then-passing test; an eslint ban on console.* in lib/privacy; prompts and schemas as versioned files with golden outputs; a prompt change is a PR that shows harness deltas; .env.example committed; one commit-trailer policy decided in week 1.

**Observability.** @ai-sdk/otel → Langfuse Cloud Hobby (free, 50k units per month, 2 users, verified) with recordInputs and recordOutputs false; traces carry ids, model ids, tokens, cost, latency and safety outcome; feedback table (thumbs, reason, failure tag) from day one; evals in earnest after about 50 real sessions, the bake-off before that.

**Spend caps.** Vertex budget and quota; Anthropic console limit (Start tier $500 per month cap, verified); OpenAI project budget (Build tier $500 per month, secondary); per-user daily cap (default 24 renders) and per-session cap in the DB; render ids enforced server-side; a global KILL_SWITCH env; Upstash rate limit by user; alerts at $25 and $35 of a $40 monthly budget. Stagger demo evenings, since Gemini's reported $10-per-10-minute spend rate and about 20 IPM are secondary.

## 8. Privacy and legal posture

Tiers: Tier 0 on device (decode, EXIF strip, gate, crop, landmarks, masks, descriptors) before consent; Tier 1 ephemeral cloud by default (cropped JPEG inline base64 → route handler in memory → vendor → browser; no bucket, no first-party storage; only profile text, consent, feedback and cost rows persist); Tier 2 opt-in encrypted auto-expiring library deferred to an owner decision. Never the Gemini free tier; billing attached before the first photo. Landmarks are Article 4(14) biometric data under the ICO reading but not Article 9 (not for identification); never persisted or transmitted. The confirmed profile stores bucketed enums, not raw ratios, and sits inside the published retention schedule and delete-everything; delete-everything keeps the hashed consent proof and a deletion record.

Law: BIPA reaches try-on "scans" (MAC motion to dismiss denied June 2026, date secondary); a private entity includes individuals, so the owner is covered; P.A. 103-769 makes an electronic signature a written release and caps recovery at one per person per method (retroactive per Gregg, 7th Cir., 2026-04-01). The same consent fits Washington MHMDA (biometric data as consumer health data, no revenue floor) and Colorado (photos excluded unless used for identification). GDPR/UK: the household exemption is unlikely to cover a service run for others with third-party processors, so publish an Article 13 notice, keep a light DPIA in the repo, execute the Anthropic DPA and Google Cloud data processing terms, and use SCCs for EU/UK friends. EU AI Act Article 50 applies since 2026-08-02 (the Omnibus grandfathers only 50(2) marking until 2026-12-02); friends' personal use is outside 50(4), but face modification is a listed marking example and stylist exports are professional-facing, so every result carries a visible "AI-edited preview" label; provider marks survive only where the export is unmodified provider bytes, paste-back composites lose C2PA and rely on the label, and no re-signing infrastructure is built. TAKE IT DOWN: an invite-only app very likely is not a covered platform (it must "serve the public"); implement report/flag and 48-hour removal anyway once any sharing exists. Design out misuse: own face only, single face, live selfie default, reference photos hair-cropped on device, adults only, no renders of others. Vendor posture is in Section 5; the unresolved Vertex abuse-log window (30 vs 90 days) is read on the primary page before consent copy is written. Consent is shaped to Apple DPLA 3.3.3(K), Guideline 5.1.2(i) (last updated 2026-10-16) and Play's disclosure-immediately-before-consent rule even on web, so a native shell would need no redesign; the Android 2026-08-25 guidance (full-access reviewer account, documented safety tests) is noted for that day.

Textured-hair and grey-hair subsets reported separately with their own thresholds, and a "this did not work for my hair" feedback tag that blocks a provider from becoming default. Analysis consistency: two separate calls per photo (two prompt variants, since temperature is unavailable on 5.x) and two crops, scored on enums and taxonomy IDs. Safety-prompt tests documented in the repo.

  rejected: XState from day one; XState 6.0.0-alpha; Stately Agent; boolean-flag state; Redux-style stores.

- **Async jobs** -> None in the MVP: one route handler per candidate streaming SSE, a renders row per call for idempotency, status, cost and outcome; Screen Wake Lock; measured backgrounded-tab loss. Add Trigger.dev (20 concurrent free) with bucket keys, never bytes, only if loss or provider duration forces it. [medium]
  rejected: Single handler with Promise.all fan-out (drops all paid renders on Safari backgrounding); Inngest Free (5 concurrent steps queue a demo); Vercel Workflow DevKit (pricing unverified); event bus, CQRS, event sourcing, Temporal; transient R2 staging in the MVP (contradicts keep-nothing posture).
  why: Two to six provider calls per screen do not need a runner; a per-candidate request bounds the loss to one ~$0.04 render; introducing a runner adds a fourth-party processor that must then be named in consent.
- **Primary editor** -> gemini-nano-banana-2.1 on Vertex AI (billing, cacheConfig disableCache=true, region recorded, abuse-monitoring exception requested) at 1K for candidates; gemini-3-pro-image for the hero; gpt-image-2.5-sunburst as an opt-in, consent-listed fallback; FLUX 3 Image via fal or self-hosted Klein as the Phase 3 private path, Ring B only until terms are read. [medium]
  rejected: Gemini Developer API paid tier (55-day abuse logs, ZDR eligibility unknown); OpenAI as default (30-day logs, ZDR sales-gated, whole-portrait re-rendering reports); BFL direct (training terms unread); Seedream 5.0 Pro (86-110 s, SG/MY processing, terms unreachable); YouCam (30-day retention, ~$0.09-0.11 per render unverified).

  rejected: Haiku 5.5 everywhere (false economy: analysis is cents and its 1A-4C and face JSON quality is untested); Opus 5.5 default (2x cost, untested gain); Gemini Flash (55-day logs on the Developer API); Fable 5.1 and Mythos (Covered Models with mandatory 30-day retention, no ZDR).

[high]
  rejected: Transient R2 bucket for crops (an at-rest first-party copy plus an extra processor, avoidable at ten users); server-side render library by default; Gemini free tier (trains, human review); per-user vendor allowlist beyond one OpenAI toggle (friction, non-comparable results).
  why: The only retention design in which the consent can truthfully say nothing is stored on the owner's servers; matches Apple's 'transient processing is not collected' story and the owner's most-private-that-works default.
- **Auth** -> better-auth 1.7.12 magic link with disableSignUp and an email allowlist; adults-only self-declaration at consent; @better-auth/passkey 1.7.12 added once friends are onboarded. [high]

  why: Invite-only is the product's safety boundary (own face only, no public sharing); magic links are the lowest-friction path for a friend group and keep no third-party identity provider in the data flow.
- **Hosting** -> Vercel Hobby for the friends phase, with Cloudflare Workers (verified free tier) as a drop-in if the non-commercial fair-use reading, the 4.5 MB body limit or function duration bite; payloads kept under ~1.5 MB. [medium]
  rejected: Vercel Pro up front (~$20/seat, unverified); Cloudflare first (more Next.js setup); Fly or a VPS (ops burden for a solo developer).
  why: Zero cost and the fastest deploy loop; the server is a thin proxy with no image storage, so a host move is a one-week change with no architectural impact.
- **Storage** -> Postgres on a free tier (Neon or Supabase) with Drizzle for users, consents (hash plus notice text), profiles as bucketed enums, renders, events, feedback and spend ledger, never bytes; renders and history in IndexedDB plus save-to-Photos; no blob store. [high]
  rejected: Vercel Blob or R2 for photos or renders in the MVP; Supabase storage with signed URLs (cannot be revoked early); storing raw geometry ratios or landmarks.
  why: Keeps every face byte off the owner's infrastructure, makes delete-everything trivial, and keeps BIPA/GDPR exposure to text records inside a published retention schedule.
- **Observability** -> @ai-sdk/otel to Langfuse Cloud Hobby (free, 50k units/month, verified) with recordInputs and recordOutputs false; cost ledger from measured usage; feedback table from day one; alerts at $25/$35 of a $40 budget; provider-side caps and a kill switch. [high]
  rejected: Helicone (paid tiers and maintenance-mode claim unverified); Braintrust ($249/month); self-hosted Langfuse now (ops burden); any tracing that records prompts or images.
  why: Free at friends scale, supports datasets and scores for later evals, and the input/output switch keeps face data out of traces; spend caps, not rate limits, are the real scaling wall.
- **Eval approach** -> Phase 0 measures real cost, latency and refusals; Phase 1 uses the feedback table and a two-call consistency probe; Phase 2 freezes a hashed golden set, builds the offline harness (ArcFace floors, masked LPIPS, mask IoU, VLM rubric), runs a two-ring bake-off (friends' photos only to vetted vendors, SFHQ synthetic to the rest) and a blind friend A/B; a prompt change is a PR with harness deltas; textured-hair subset has its own thresholds. [high]
  rejected: Harness-before-product (friends see nothing until week 9); runtime ArcFace ranking (non-commercial weights, biometric verification under BIPA/GDPR); FID at friend scale; sending friends' faces to unvetted vendors in the bake-off; CLIP as a hairstyle matcher.
  why: No independent identity benchmark exists for any 2026 editor, so in-house measurement on consented photos is the only evidence; sequencing it after the walking skeleton keeps a solo developer shipping while still gating every renderer and prompt decision.

## What would change the plan
- An independent controlled hair-only identity benchmark appears (or the Phase 2 bake-off on friends' photos shows it) with another vetted editor clearly ahead of Nano Banana 2.1 on identity pass rate or the textured-hair subset: swap the primary through the HairEditProvider interface after a harness re-run and update consent copy.
- Vertex declines the abuse-monitoring exception for a hobby project, gemini-nano-banana-2.1 is unavailable in any acceptable region, or the primary docs state a 90-day abuse-log window with no way out: fall back to the global endpoint, and if the retention story is still unacceptable promote self-hosted FLUX 3 Image Klein or fal-hosted FLUX 3 (once fal's partner-model flag and DPA are read) earlier than Phase 3.
- Measured backgrounded-tab render loss exceeds a few percent, or a provider's latency exceeds the function limit: introduce one durable runner (Trigger.dev) with private-bucket keys and delete-on-completion, and name that processor in the consent notice.
- Vercel Hobby's non-commercial terms or its body and duration limits bite in Phase 0: move to Cloudflare Workers; nothing in the architecture changes.
- Nano Banana latency p95 measured in Phase 0 is above about 30 s: cut candidates per look from two to one plus a bald-first chain only when needed, lean harder on progressive reveal, and reconsider a faster tier.
- A vendor ships live photoreal hairstyle replacement that holds identity on video: revisit the native shell and Tier 4, reusing the client-agnostic API.
- The owner decides to monetize, open the app beyond friends, or charge anything: licensing walls (InsightFace, StyleGAN2 FFHQ weights, research datasets, FLUX Kommunity revenue threshold), Vercel Pro, TAKE IT DOWN covered-platform analysis, formal DPAs and SCCs, and BIPA exposure at scale all move from notes to blockers.
- The Anthropic AUP revision effective 2026-11-12 removes the consent carve-out for analysis, or Sonnet 5.5 / Haiku 5.5 become Covered Models with mandatory retention: move analysis to Gemini on Vertex (paid, cache disabled) and re-run the consistency probe.
- OpenAI grants self-serve ZDR or Modified Abuse Monitoring for image edits, or Google publishes Developer API ZDR eligibility for small projects: the fallback could become a default-eligible renderer and the Developer API could be reconsidered.
- H-Adapter or DPF release code under a permissive licence, or HairFastGAN's StyleGAN2 dependency is replaced: the reference-hair specialist and the 'inspired by' framing can be revisited.
- The MediaPipe GPU delegate measures under about 8 fps for the hair mask on friends' phones, or the iOS 27 home-screen camera path still rotates frames: the live recolor preview is cut and the viewfinder stays behind its flag.
- Friends include anyone under 18, or several EEA/UK friends join: under-18s remain excluded entirely (Gemini API terms, no age estimation), and EEA/UK friends require the paid tier, an Article 13 notice and SCCs before their first photo.
- The Phase 2 blind A/B fails the textured-hair subset on every vetted vendor: ship with an explicit in-app limitation for those curl patterns, keep the bald-first and stylist-reviewed bank, and prioritise a specialist experiment in Phase 3 over reference transfer.
