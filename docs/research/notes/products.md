Takeaway: the "what suits me" category exists but is (a) separate from rendering, (b) mostly geometry-rule-based (5–9 face shapes, 1990s magazine rules), and (c) drifting toward "looksmaxxing" scoring (attractiveness, PSL score) — a tone the owner's friends-group app can deliberately avoid.

## 2. Output quality and complaint themes

Could not read store reviews this session. The following themes are supported by evidence in the open code/papers above and by commonly reported review themes `[training knowledge, UNVERIFIED]`:

1. **"It changed my face / doesn't look like me."** Generative restyles (Flux Kontext, Gemini image, SD-based) drift identity; every community prompt list is dominated by "keep the face 100% identical" instructions (https://github.com/ZeroLu/awesome-nanobanana-pro). GAN/latent methods (HairFastGAN, HairCLIP) keep identity better but need FFHQ-style aligned frontal crops and look "2021".
2. **Unrealistic hair, wrong hairline, bald-cap artifacts.** Two-stage methods literally make you bald first (Stable-Hair, HairPort); failures there propagate.
3. **Non-frontal, glasses, hats, multiple faces fail.** HairPort and FaceFrame both gate to a single frontal face; face-metrics refuses >8° yaw. Users rarely take ideal photos.
4. **Textured/coiled hair and men's styles are underserved.** Figaro-1k has 150 images per class for kinky/braids/dreadlocks; FaceFrame admits "coiled-texture and medium/long men's combinations return no matches"; catalogs skew female.
5. **Paywall after one free try; credits that expire; weekly subs.** The market norm is 3 free generations then credits (hairgen-io) or weekly/annual subs; CapCut sells 149 credits for $1.49 and notes credits expire in 2 years (Mobbin screen https://mobbin.com/screens/c562545d-32ff-43fd-9093-9d47f9dcc20b).
6. **Slow (10–60 s) with no progress** — mitigated by staged progress messaging (hairgen-io's 4 phases; Mobbin examples in §4).
7. **Catalog fatigue: "too few styles" / "all the same styles" / "no way to say shorter or more curl".** None of the catalog tools exposes a refine loop beyond "regenerate".
8. **Privacy unease** — FaceApp 2019 is the canonical scare; the DPLA now defines an *uploaded photo* as Face Data (see §3), so "we send your face to a third-party model" must be disclosed and consented.

## 3. Platform / policy items that affect a face-photo app (verified unless marked)

### Apple App Review Guidelines (opened 2026-10-20) https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- 5.1.1(iii) Data Minimization: "Where possible, use the out-of-process picker or a share sheet rather than requesting full access to protected resources like Photos."
- 5.1.1(ii) Permission: consent for data collection; "Paid functionality must not be dependent on or require a user to grant access to this data."
- 5.1.1(v): if there are no significant account features, let people use it without a login; if accounts exist, offer in-app account deletion.
- **5.1.2(i): "You must clearly disclose where personal data will be shared with third parties, including with third-party AI, and obtain explicit permission before doing so."** (Direct hit for calling Gemini/OpenAI/fal with a selfie.)
- 5.1.2(ii): data collected for one purpose may not be repurposed without further consent.
- 5.1.2(vi): data "from depth and/or facial mapping tools (such as ARKit, Camera APIs, or Photo APIs) may not be used for marketing, advertising or use-based data mining, including by third parties."
- 2.5.13: facial recognition for *authentication* must use LocalAuthentication, not ARKit (not relevant unless you add face login).
- 1.2: "AI content creation apps" fall under user-generated-content rules (filtering, reporting, blocking, contact) — relevant if friends can post/vote.

### Apple Developer Program License Agreement §3.3.3(K) Face Data (opened 2026-10-20; DPLA last updated 2026-08-18) https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/
- Definition (§1.2): "Face Data" means information related to human faces (e.g., face mesh data, facial map data, face modeling data, facial coordinates or facial landmark data, **including data from an uploaded photo**) ... or that is provided by a user in or through an Application (e.g., uploads for a facial analysis service)."
- Verbatim obligations: use Face Data "only to provide a service or function that is directly relevant to the use of the Application"; "inform users of Your intended uses and disclosures of Face Data ... and obtain clear and conspicuous consent from such users before any collection or use"; no advertising/marketing/profiling; no transfer to analytics providers/data brokers; "Face Data may not be shared or transferred off the user's device unless You have obtained clear and conspicuous consent for the transfer and the Face Data is used only in fulfilling a specific service or function ... You agree to require that Your service providers use Face Data only to the limited extent consented to."

### Apple App Privacy Details (nutrition label) https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- "Collect" = transmitting data off device in a way that lets you/partners access it longer than needed to service the request in real time. "Data that is processed only on device is not 'collected'." Data sent to a server and "immediately discarded after servicing the request" need not be disclosed.
- If a feature uploads photos you must declare "Photos or Videos" (User Content); "Sensitive Info" includes biometric data.

### Apple photo access
- PHPickerViewController (iOS 14+) is out-of-process and needs no photo permission; limited library access exists for apps that insist on PHPhotoLibrary. (Doc page https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photokit/delivering-an-enhanced-privacy-experience-in-your-photos-app rendered empty via fetch; statement from the fetched guideline 5.1.1(iii) plus training knowledge.)

### Android / Google Play
- Photo picker: no permission; native on Android 11+ with Google System Updates; backport to Android 4.4–10 via Play services; persisted grants capped at 5,000. https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/shared/photopicker
- Android 14 "Selected Photos Access" (`READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED`) for apps targeting API 34 that keep their own gallery picker. https://developer.android.com/about/versions/14/changes/partial-photo-video-access
- Play policy pages (support.google.com) were unreachable. `[UNVERIFIED this session, from training knowledge]`: Play's Photo and Video Permissions policy restricts `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES/VIDEO` to apps whose core purpose needs broad library access and requires a declaration (enforced through 2025) — use the photo picker; User Data policy requires prominent disclosure + runtime consent before collecting "personal and sensitive user data" (biometric/face data is listed), an accurate Data safety section, and in-app + web account deletion if accounts exist. Policy hub: https://play.google/developer-content-policy/ ; current update page per developer.android.com: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17134731
- Illinois BIPA `[UNVERIFIED this session]`: "biometric identifier" excludes photographs but courts treat face-geometry scans derived from photos as covered; statutory damages $1,000/$5,000 per violation; 2024 amendment limits accrual to per-person. Relevant if the app is ever distributed beyond friends; irrelevant for a private friends build but worth a one-line consent anyway.

## 6. White space the owner's app can own (synthesis)
1. **"What suits my face" as the primary loop, with rendering as proof** — every catalog tool starts from a grid; face-shape sites stop at rules. Combine on-device face metrics (face-metrics / MediaPipe 478 points, pose-gated) with a vision-language model critique that explains *why* (jaw, forehead, hair density, texture) and renders only the top candidates. Hair_gemini already shows the model can both render and critique.
2. **Refine loop with vocabulary** ("shorter", "more curl", "5-minute styling", "keep my texture") — no catalog tool exposes this beyond Regenerate. Model it as a state machine over a style spec (length, texture, parting, fringe, volume, color) rather than free text.
3. **Stylist handoff sheet** — HairPath/ManeRoute is the only thing close: reference image + plain-language "what to ask for" + barber reply link. Worth copying its barber Can-do/Let's-talk/Not-yet loop.
4. **Friends-group features** — only Hair Zapp claims "community feedback"; Alta shows a friends feed works for try-ons. A private group with A/B votes (YouTube/Paired/Grok patterns) is cheap and unoccupied.
5. **Honest fit feedback** — most tools flatter; a "this will take 25 minutes every morning" or "needs more density than you have" note is a differentiator, provided it never becomes attractiveness scoring (the looksmaxxing drift in §1.4 is a cautionary tale).
6. **Privacy as a feature** — the DPLA defines uploaded selfies as Face Data; the cleanest story is: analysis on device (MediaPipe / Apple hair matte), rendering via a server that discards images (transient = not "collected" per Apple), explicit per-upload consent naming the third-party model, no gallery permission (pickers), user-visible delete. hairgen-io's 24-hour auto-delete and fade-try-on's delete-after-each-task are concrete references.
7. **Textured hair and men's styles** — explicit gap across catalogs and datasets; a curated reference set with coil/kinky/braids/locs and men's medium/long would be unusual.
8. **Live video try-on**: the only live thing anyone ships is hair *color* (segmentation + recolor; MediaPipe/Snap/Apple matte). Photoreal live *restyle* does not exist in any product found; HairPort needs minutes on a 24 GB GPU. Treat live restyle as out of scope; live recolor + a "freeze frame → render" handoff is realistic.
9. **Reference-photo hair transfer** ("put this person's hair on me") exists in research (HairFastGAN <1 s/V100 but GAN-era quality; Stable-Hair; HairPort) and in products as "upload a reference" (StyleMirror AI, doosanddonts, Nano Banana two-image prompts). Quality is the risk, not availability.
