## Summary

Re-verified the image-editing price/policy inputs with 15 searches plus direct fetches. Primary wins: Google's Vertex pricing page (cloud.google.com, fetched 2026-10-20) gives the full Nano Banana token ladders, settling Nano Banana 2.1 at $0.034/1K (1,120 tok), $0.05/2K (1,680), $0.113/4K (3,780), input images 1,120 tokens ($0.00168), batch $15/1M; Nano Banana 2 at $0.045/512, $0.067/1K, $0.101/2K, $0.15/4K; Pro at $0.134 (1K-2K) / $0.24 (4K) with 560 input tokens.
