# Gap-fill: product reviews, prices and the live-video question

_Second-pass research lane `gap-products-reviews` (2026-10-20), run with a fresh web-search budget (max 15 searches) to re-check items the first sweep could not verify. Many vendor hosts stayed blocked, so several numbers are still marked unverified._

Long-form notes: [notes/gap-products-reviews.md](../notes/gap-products-reviews.md)

## Summary

Gap-fill for the products lane under a 15-search cap with every consumer/vendor host blocked by the proxy (apps.apple.com, play.google.com, perfectcorp.com, docs.perfectcorp.com, review aggregators, Wayback all unreachable; reddit.com refused by the search API). Consumer numbers therefore come from search-result snippets of third-party aggregators; YouCam API numbers come from GitHub-hosted mirrors (a captured live feature-cost response from 2026-10-10, the official npm CLI README published 2026-10-08, and two independent third-party reads of the pricing page). Key results: (1) Ratings/prices (secondary): YouCam Makeup App Store 4.7 / Play 4.1 (counts not obtained), complaint themes = paywall on essentials/saving, billing/cancellation, uncanny 'synthetic mask'; FaceApp ~4.7 with ~1.7M ratings, Pro price unpublished (~$19.99/yr tracker estimate, unverified); Facetune 4.6 (338-362K) App Store / 4.5 (765K) Play, $25/mo, $40/quarter, $77.99/yr; HairTry 3.6-3.9 stars (72-1,756 reviews), $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr, imports a style from Instagram/Pinterest; hairstyleai.com $9 one-time per 'photoshoot' (30 styles x 4 poses = 120 photos, ~1 hour, US-hosted, no free plan); The Face Report sells one SKU, 'Full Face Report' $17.49 one-time including 6 AI hairstyle try-ons and 12-season colour analysis, no 'Hairstyle Pack' found; Hair by AILab, Epik, Vivid AI, Fotor prices/ratings not obtainable. (2) No first-hand Reddit evidence could be gathered; the only textured-hair test found (photoaistudio, self-interested) says most apps flatten curls or draw geometric spirals and one app always fails 4C; Nano Banana user tests say it struggles with updos and drifts after ~4 chained edits and watermarks outputs; TikTok evidence is limited to colour filters (AI hair dye split filter whose advice changes day to day) and viral Gemini prompts. (3) No product ships live photoreal hairstyle replacement on video in 2026: YouCam live = preset AR overlays, Snapchat = colour lenses, Decart Lucy = generic real-time V2V (sub-40 ms, 30 fps, $0.02/s) with a clothing VTON product but no hair product and identity changes 'by design'; YouCam API has no hair video endpoint. (4) L'Oreal Style My Hair has no 2026 status signal (launch-era coverage, undated L'Oreal China page, ModiFace patent granted 2026-06-16); Google Doppl is clothing-only with no hair support. (5) YouCam API: hairstyle v2.1 (preset or custom reference) = 2 units, hair colour = 1, bangs/extension/wavy = 1, volume = 2, beard = 2, hair type/length/frizz detection = 2 each, density = 1, Face Attribute & Ratio 10-30, Skin Analysis 9-22; USD per unit ~$0.055 pay-as-you-go (1-year validity) or ~$0.047 (plan basis unstated), i.e. ~$0.09-0.11 per hairstyle render; 1,000 free units via hackathon/affiliate codes, ~40-unit sandbox otherwise; 30-day retention with delete endpoint; ToS bans using outputs to build training datasets.
