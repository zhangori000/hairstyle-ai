# Product landscape, UX patterns and app-store rules

_Research lane `products` from the 2026-10-20 sweep. Written by a research agent, then fact-checked by independent verifier agents; the verification section at the end records every correction. Numbers carry an as-of date and a source. Items marked unverified could not be confirmed from this sandbox (many vendor sites were blocked) and should be re-checked on the live page before you rely on them._

Long-form notes with every URL: [notes/products.md](../notes/products.md)

## Summary

Constraint: the shared WebSearch budget was exhausted before this lane began, and the proxy only resolved github.com, developer.apple.com and developer.android.com; app stores, Reddit/HN, vendor sites and Play policy pages were unreachable. Everything from GitHub READMEs, Apple/Android primary docs and Mobbin screenshots is verified; store ratings, review complaints and most consumer prices are flagged unverified and need a follow-up run with search enabled.

Landscape: ~50 "AI hairstyle changer" products exist (curated list, Sept 2025). Nearly all share one loop: upload a selfie, pick from a catalog (10 to 140+ styles), pay credits or a weekly/annual sub. Incumbents: Perfect Corp YouCam (live AR hair color, generative restyle, web generator with 140+ styles, plus a B2B API), FaceApp, Facetune, Fotor, BeautyPlus/AirBrush/LightX/Picsart/MyEdit/Epik, L'Oréal Style My Hair (ModiFace), long-tail web tools, and a separate "face-shape analyzer" category (The Face Report, YouCam Face Shape Detector, KnowMyFace, becometen/face-metrics, FaceFrame) that gives rules but does not render. Google Doppl/Search try-on are clothing; Gemini image ("Nano Banana") is what 2025-26 indie apps call for identity-preserving restyles. Live video exists only as hair recolor (MediaPipe/Snap/Apple hair matte); no product ships photoreal live restyle (HairPort needs 24 GB VRAM and minutes per pair).

Policy (verified): Apple App Review 5.1.2(i) now requires clear disclosure and explicit permission before sharing personal data with third-party AI; the DPLA 3.3.3(K) defines an uploaded photo as Face Data, requires clear and conspicuous consent before any collection/use and before any off-device transfer, and binds service providers; 5.1.2(vi) bans marketing use of camera/photo-derived face data; 5.1.1(iii) says use the out-of-process picker. Apple's privacy label treats on-device processing and transient server processing as "not collected". Android: photo picker needs no permission; Android 14 selected-photos access.

UX patterns (Mobbin): mode chooser (live/photo/roll), before-after split slider, plain-language face-data consent (Warby Parker), saved "Me!" face (Pinterest), friends feed + invite (Alta), oval capture overlay with lighting meter, staged generation progress, variant grid + regenerate, A/B polls, share cards, weekly paywalls and credit packs.

White space: "what suits my face" as the primary loop with rendering as proof; a vocabulary-driven refine loop; stylist handoff sheet (only HairPath/ManeRoute does this, with a barber reply link); private friends-group votes; honest fit feedback without looksmaxxing scoring; on-device analysis + transient rendering privacy; textured hair and men's medium/long styles, which catalogs and datasets underserve.

## Recommendations

- Position the app as 'find what suits my face, then prove it' rather than another catalog: on-device face metrics (MediaPipe 478-point mesh via face-metrics-style rules with pose gating) plus a VLM critique that explains why, rendering only the top 3-5 candidates.
- Model the refine loop as a typed style spec (length, texture, parting, fringe, volume, color, styling-time budget) driven by a state machine; map feedback words ('shorter', 'more curl', '5 minutes') to spec deltas, then re-render. No competitor exposes this.
- Copy HairPath/ManeRoute's stylist handoff: reference image + plain-language 'ask for' sheet + a share link the stylist can answer (Can do / Let's talk / Not yet). Add an A/B vote link for friends (YouTube/Paired/Grok poll patterns).
- Adopt the privacy architecture Apple's rules reward: PHPicker/Android photo picker (no library permission), on-device analysis, transient server rendering that discards images (so it is 'not collected'), an explicit per-upload consent naming the third-party model (DPLA 3.3.3(K), Guideline 5.1.2(i)), visible delete, no analytics on images. Use hairgen-io's 24h auto-delete and fade-try-on's delete-after-task as concrete references.
- Scope live video to hair recolor and a freeze-frame-to-render handoff; do not promise live photoreal restyle. Keep reference-photo transfer as a later experiment (Nano Banana two-image prompt first; HairFastGAN/Stable-Hair as fallbacks).
- Build the capture screen from proven patterns: oval overlay, live lighting meter, auto-capture, good-vs-bad example photos, single-face and pose checks that ask for a retake instead of guessing.
- Deliberately cover textured/coiled hair and men's medium/long styles in the curated reference set and test images; it is a documented gap in catalogs and datasets.
- Keep honest fit feedback factual (styling time, maintenance, density needed) and avoid attractiveness scores; the face-analysis niche is drifting into looksmaxxing.
- If buying rendering, evaluate Perfect Corp's YouCam API (hair-transfer at 2 units/render, 30-day retention unless deleted) against Gemini image / fal hair-change on identity preservation with your friends' real photos; always call delete after each task.
- Re-run this lane with web search enabled to fill the unverified gaps: App Store/Play ratings and complaints for YouCam, FaceApp, Facetune, Hair by AILab, HairTry; current prices; Google Play Photo/Video permissions and July 2026 policy text; YouCam API dollar-per-unit.

- **[decision-critical]** _high confidence._ **The market default is catalog-first: upload selfie, pick from 10-140+ preset styles, pay credits or a subscription; no product found exposes a refine loop beyond 'regenerate' or starts from 'what suits my face' with rendering as proof.**  
  A Sept-2025 curated list enumerates ~50 commercial changers (YouCam web generator 'over 140 hairstyles', MyEdit 43+, Hair-Style.AI 60+, HairCut 90+, The Right Hairstyles 100+ with credit packs of 20/40/80). Face-shape sites (The Face Report, YouCam Face Shape Detector, KnowMyFace, FaceFrame) recommend by rules but do not render. Open-source SaaS clones (hairgen-io: 24 styles, 3 free generations then credits; ai-hair-style-simulator: 18 credits per render at $1=200 credits) replicate the same loop.

- **[decision-critical]** _high confidence._ **Apple now treats an uploaded selfie as 'Face Data' and requires clear, conspicuous consent before any collection/use and before any off-device transfer; App Review 5.1.2(i) separately requires explicit permission before sharing personal data with third-party AI.**  
  DPLA (updated 2026-08-18) s1.2 defines Face Data to include 'data from an uploaded photo ... uploads for a facial analysis service'; s3.3.3(K) says Face Data 'may not be shared or transferred off the user's device unless You have obtained clear and conspicuous consent for the transfer and the Face Data is used only in fulfilling a specific service or function', bans advertising/profiling use, and requires service providers to be bound. Guideline 5.1.2(i): 'You must clearly disclose where personal data will be shared with third parties, including with third-party AI, and obtain explicit permission before doing so.' 5.1.2(vi) bans marketing/data-mining use of Camera/Photo API-derived face data. 5.1.1(iii) says use the out-of-process picker rather than full Photos access.  
  Sources: <https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/>, <https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/>
- **[decision-critical]** _high confidence._ **On-device processing and transient server processing are not 'collected' under Apple's privacy label rules, which makes 'analyze on device, render via a server that discards the image' the cleanest privacy story.**  
  Apple: 'Data that is processed only on device is not collected and does not need to be disclosed'; data 'sent to your servers then immediately discarded after servicing the request' need not be disclosed either. If a feature uploads photos the 'Photos or Videos' type must be declared; 'Sensitive Info' includes biometric data. iOS 13+ also generates a hair matte on device at capture (AVSemanticSegmentationMatte hair/skin/teeth), useful for recolor and masking without any upload.  
  Sources: <https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/>, <https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2019/260/>
- **[decision-critical]** _high confidence._ **Photoreal live-video hairstyle replacement is not shipped by any product found; live try-on in the market is hair recolor via segmentation (Snap/TikTok lenses, MediaPipe web apps, Apple hair matte). Reference-photo hair transfer exists but is research-grade or slow.**  
  Open-source live hair color apps run 100% client-side with MediaPipe hair segmenter (Ys-sudo/hair-coloring-app, otdnnc/virtual-makeup, lagrandecode/myhair-ai Flutter). Products that accept a reference photo (StyleMirror AI, doosanddonts via Gemini 2.5 Flash Image) are photo-only.

- **[decision-critical]** _medium confidence._ **Perfect Corp exposes the incumbent's hair features as a task-based REST API (hair-transfer, hair-color, hair-bang, hair-ext, beard-style, hair length/type/frizziness/density detection) with unit pricing, a 30-day retention window and a delete endpoint; it is a credible buy-not-build option for rendering, at a privacy cost.**
