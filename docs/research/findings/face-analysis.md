# Face and hair analysis: landmarks, vision LLMs, the stylist brain

_Research lane `face-analysis` from the 2026-10-20 sweep. Written by a research agent, then fact-checked by independent verifier agents; the verification section at the end records every correction. Numbers carry an as-of date and a source. Items marked unverified could not be confirmed from this sandbox (many vendor sites were blocked) and should be re-checked on the live page before you rely on them._

Long-form notes with every URL: [notes/face-analysis.md](../notes/face-analysis.md)

## Summary

Caveat: the shared WebSearch budget was exhausted before this lane ran, and the sandbox blocked openai.com, ai.google.dev, arXiv, Wikipedia, Kaggle, HF and journals; findings come from direct fetches of primary sources (Anthropic docs, Apple docs, MediaPipe model cards, npm/PyPI, GitHub) plus flagged recall.

Core conclusion: split the problem. Geometry (face proportions, pose, hair mask, hair colour) should come from on-device landmarks, and semantics (hair length, curl pattern, bangs, part, beard, glasses, hairline) from a vision LLM with structured output; computed ratios are injected into the prompt as text. Stylist face-shape categories are a folk taxonomy with no standard definitions: the hobby/commercial implementations found all invent their thresholds, two key distinctions (round vs square, heart vs diamond) depend on jaw angle/chin shape, and MediaPipe's own landmark error (2.6-3.2% of inter-ocular distance) is at the noise floor of a "3% tolerance" rule, so represent face shape as continuous ratios with soft top-2 labels averaged over video frames, never a hard class. Published ML classifiers plateau around 84% on a celebrity-women dataset (recall).

On-device: MediaPipe Face Landmarker (478 points, 52 blendshapes, transformation matrix; 3.76 MB; @mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.1.1 published 2026-10-14, Apache-2.0) plus the 0.78 MB hair segmenter or 16 MB multiclass segmenter cover web/Android/iOS; Apple gives 76-point landmarks with per-point precision, a 0-1 face capture-quality score, and a capture-time hair matte (iOS 13+). Avoid dlib's 68-point model (iBUG licence forbids commercial use) and InsightFace model packs (non-commercial); face-api.js is unmaintained since 2020.

VLM cost is negligible: a 1536x2048 photo is 4070 Claude visual tokens (28px patches, hi-res tier), so a full analysis is about $0.0009 on Haiku 5.5, $0.017 on Sonnet 5.5, $0.034 on Opus 5.5; Gemini 3.8 Flash counts a fixed ~1080 tokens per image (~$0.0045/analysis at secondary-source prices). Claude structured outputs are GA with constrained decoding but require additionalProperties:false, allow no numeric min/max and limited optional fields; schema sketches are in the notes. Anthropic's AUP bans identification, biometric categorisation, emotion/race inference and body ranking but explicitly allows analysis the person agreed to; never infer gender/age/ethnicity, ask the user. Measured fairness gap: the hair/skin segmenter scores 71.9 IoU on Monk tones 9-10 vs 81.7 on tones 1-2. Hair typing (1A-4C) is contested and VLMs likely under-differentiate coily textures; require user confirmation of every field. Recommended stylist brain: typed catalog + deterministic feasibility/effect filter + LLM ranking/explanation, with a RefinementIntent enum driving the refine state machine. Test data: FairFace (CC BY 4.0) for bias probes; FaceSynthetics/CelebAMask-HQ/FFHQ for internal eval only (non-commercial).

## Recommendations

- Split analysis into deterministic on-device geometry (MediaPipe Face Landmarker 478 points + hair segmenter, or Apple Vision/AVFoundation on iOS) and a single vision-LLM semantic pass with structured output; inject the computed ratios into the prompt as text instead of asking the model to estimate proportions.
- Represent face shape as continuous features (length/width, jaw/cheek, forehead/cheek, chin taper, gonial angle) with soft top-2 labels and a confidence derived from pose gate and frame-to-frame spread; never show a single hard class, and let the catalog rules consume geometry effects (adds height, softens jaw, covers forehead) rather than shape names.
- Gate capture before measuring: single face, |yaw| <= ~8-10 deg, roll-corrected, face >= 15% of frame, sharpness/lighting check (Apple faceCaptureQuality on iOS); average ratios over ~30 video frames; request an optional hair-pulled-back shot for hairline and ears and a shoulders-in-frame shot for neck length.
- Design the FaceHairProfile schema flat and all-required with 'unknown' enum members, per-field confidence and evidence strings, provenance (measured | model | user), and a mandatory user confirm/fix step; store the profile text, not photos.
- Never ask any model to infer gender, age, ethnicity, emotion or attractiveness; collect pronouns/presentation goals from the user; use neutral, non-ranking language; treat a third party's reference photo as hair-only input.
- Start with Claude Haiku 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5 for the attribute pass (sub-cent to ~2 cents per photo), pre-downscale photos to ~1024 px long edge, use prompt caching for the stylist system prompt/catalog, and run a 20-photo agreement + refusal-rate test across Claude/Gemini/GPT before locking a vendor; avoid Fable 5.1 if zero-data-retention matters.
- Do not use dlib's 68-point model or InsightFace model packs in anything that could become commercial; prefer Apache-2.0 MediaPipe and Apple's built-in Vision APIs; skip face-api.js (unmaintained since 2020).
- Make hair typing user-confirmed: show picture-based curl-pattern choices (1A-4C plus 'mixed'), ask for stretched length and porosity/dry-time, flag when photographed hair looks heat- or chemically styled, and expect VLM under-differentiation of coily textures.
- Treat skin undertone as a user-selected input (Monk 1-10 swatches or warm/neutral/cool) with the VLM's guess shown only as a low-confidence suggestion; phone white balance makes photo-based undertone unreliable.
- Build the stylist brain as a versioned typed catalog + deterministic feasibility/maintenance/effect filter + LLM ranking and explanation (structured output), with an explicit RefinementIntent enum driving the refine state machine; allow at most one flagged 'wildcard' outside the catalog.
- Create a regression harness now: confirmed friend profiles as fixtures, a FairFace-based bias/abstention probe, and a segmentation sanity check on braids/locs/hats using FaceSynthetics; re-run on every prompt or model change.

## Findings (16)

- **[decision-critical]** _high confidence._ **Stylist face-shape categories have no standard definition; implementations invent thresholds and two key distinctions (round vs square, heart vs diamond) are not derivable from the four width/length measurements.**  
  The face-shape-measurements repo encodes the standard guide rules (length>width -> oval/oblong; length~width -> round/square; widest cheekbones -> oval/diamond; widest forehead -> heart; widest jaw -> triangle) and states its 3% 'roughly equal' tolerance is 'the tool's own parameter, not a figure from any study' and that round-vs-square and heart-vs-diamond 'depend on jaw angle and chin shape, which measurements can't capture'. Commercial detectors listed in awesome-face-shape-analysis use 5 to 9 classes. Design consequence: emit continuous ratios plus gonial angle and soft top-2 labels, not a hard class.  
  Sources: <https://github.com/heroin546-afk/face-shape-measurements>, <https://github.com/dicktracey909/awesome-face-shape-analysis>, <https://github.com/MartinSaraka/face-metrics>
- **[decision-critical]** _high confidence._ **MediaPipe Face Mesh V2 landmark error is 2.62% of inter-ocular distance (tracking) / 3.24% (re-acquisition), roughly the human annotator discrepancy (2.56%), i.e. about 1.5-2 mm per point; single-frame hard classification sits at the noise floor.**  
  Per-region error 2.06-3.27% across 17 UN geographic subregions; per-Fitzpatrick skin tone 2.49-2.90%; gender gap 0.01%. Model is a MobileNetV2-like CNN on a 256x256 face crop with 25% margin, outputs 478 3D landmarks + presence score; not suitable for faces turned >80 deg, inclined >8 deg, <50% visible. Averaging over video frames and gating on pose (yaw <= ~8 deg, roll-corrected about the 10->152 line, face >= 15% of frame) is what the face-metrics library does.  
  Sources: <https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-assets/Model%20Card%20MediaPipe%20Face%20Mesh%20V2.pdf>, <https://github.com/MartinSaraka/face-metrics>
- _high confidence._ **The exact MediaPipe face-oval landmark indices are 10,338,297,332,284,251,389,356,454,323,361,288,397,365,379,378,400,377,152,148,176,149,150,136,172,58,132,93,234,127,162,21,54,103,67,109 (closed loop), giving everything needed for length (10-152), cheek width (234-454), jaw width (172-397), forehead width (54-284), chin taper (148-377) and gonial angle.**  
  Verified from face_mesh_connections.py (Apache-2.0). Normalised coordinates must be multiplied by the image aspect ratio before measuring. The canonical face model in the face-transform module is metric in centimetres; iris landmarks (468-477) allow a mm estimate via mean iris diameter. The mesh's top point (10) is on the upper forehead, not the hairline, so forehead height/hairline must come from a hair mask, the VLM, or the user.  
  Sources: <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/master/mediapipe/python/solutions/face_mesh_connections.py>, <https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/docs/solutions/face_mesh.md>
- _high confidence._ **Claude images cost ceil(w/28) x ceil(h/28) visual tokens; all current models are on the high-resolution tier (max 2576 px long edge, max 4784 tokens), so a 1536x2048 selfie is 4070 tokens and a full analysis call costs about $0.0009 (Haiku 5.5), $0.017 (Sonnet 5.5), $0.034 (Opus 5.5), $0.086 (Fable 5.1).**  
  Reference resize algorithm is published (binary search on long edge, pad to multiple of 28). Limits: 10 MB per image base64, 8000x8000 px, 600 images/request on 1M-context models, stricter <=2000 px per image above 20 images, 32 MB request. Images are ephemeral, metadata not read, not used for training. Pre-downscaling to ~1024 px long edge cuts image tokens 3-4x. Batch API halves prices; cache reads are 0.05x on Opus/Sonnet 5.5. Fable 5.1 is not ZDR-eligible (30-day retention required).  
  Sources: <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/vision.md>, <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/vision-coordinates.md>, <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.md>
- _high confidence._ **Claude structured outputs (output_config.format json_schema) are GA with constrained decoding on all current models, but schemas must set additionalProperties:false, cannot use numeric min/max or string length limits, allow at most 24 optional and 16 union-typed parameters, and enum casing may drift.**  
  SDK helpers: Python client.messages.parse(output_format=PydanticModel), TypeScript zodOutputFormat with messages.parse(); helpers strip unsupported constraints into descriptions and validate locally. Changing the format invalidates the prompt cache; first use of a schema adds grammar compilation latency (cached 24 h); a refusal stop reason may yield non-conforming output. Forced tool_choice is a 400 on Fable 5.1/Opus 5.5/Sonnet 5.5, so structured outputs are the way to get JSON. Design the analysis schema flat, all-required, with 'unknown' enum members.  
  Sources: <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs.md>
- **[decision-critical]** _high confidence._ **Anthropic's AUP prohibits facial recognition/identification, biometric categorisation, emotion inference, inferring race/religion/politics, and 'shaming, ranking, or critiquing people's bodies based on their size or shape', but states it 'does not prohibit analysis or tracking that individuals have agreed to'; Claude also refuses to name people in images.**  
  Consented self-analysis for styling is in scope, but the prompt must not ask for gender, age, ethnicity, attractiveness or emotion, must avoid ranking language, and if a reference photo of another person is uploaded only the hair should be analysed. Current Claude models run safety classifiers that can return stop_reason 'refusal'; refusal rate on real face photos must be measured early and the server-side fallbacks beta considered.  
  Sources: <https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup>, <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/vision.md>
- **[decision-critical]** _high confidence._ **MediaPipe's multiclass selfie segmenter (the on-device hair-mask option) has a measurable skin-tone fairness gap: mean IoU 71.86 on Monk tones 9-10 vs 81.70 on tones 1-2 at 512x512 (68.25 vs 77.22 at 256x256), with only 31 dark-tone eval images.**  
  Classes: background, hair, body-skin, face-skin, clothes, others; ViT-based; Apache-2.0; 16.4 MB (256 model). A separate 0.78 MB hair_segmenter.tflite exists (no model card found). Hair-length/volume estimates derived from masks will be noisier on the darkest skin tones; treat mask-derived fields as low confidence and ask the user to confirm.  
  Sources: <https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-assets/Model%20Card%20Multiclass%20Segmentation.pdf>, <https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_segmenter/selfie_multiclass_256x256/float32/latest/selfie_multiclass_256x256.tflite>
